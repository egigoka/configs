Your app might run on the iPhone Duo, but that doesn’t mean it’s ready. Apple’s foldable iPhone brings new layout configurations, toolbars that move to the sides, and a lot more to think about than just making your UI wider. In this video, I break down what iOS developers need to know to build a SwiftUI app that feels designed for Duo and why using native components can save you a lot of work.

We’ll cover adaptive layouts and size classes, vertical toolbars and overflow menus, handling the fold with reserved regions, and testing with Xcode 27.1. I also discuss widgets in Standby Mode, Apple Pencil, and drag and drop, plus a hinge interaction that could spark an entirely new app idea. Whether you’re updating an existing app or starting from scratch, you’ll learn what to rethink, what Apple handles for you, and how to avoid shipping a stretched-out iPhone app.
